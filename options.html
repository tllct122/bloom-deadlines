Bloom for Blackboard

Every deadline at a glance, and Blackboard in your colors.

1 · Your Blackboard

Open Blackboard and copy the address from the top of the window — just the start, e.g. https://learn.yourschool.edu.

Chrome will ask to let Bloom work on that one site. Bloom reads your deadlines with the Blackboard session you're already signed in to. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
