Bloom

Every deadline at a glance, and Blackboard or Canvas in your own style.

1 · Your school site

Open Blackboard or Canvas and copy the address from the top of the window — just the start, e.g. https://learn.yourschool.edu or https://yourschool.instructure.com.

Chrome will ask to let Bloom work on that one site. Bloom reads your deadlines with the session you're already signed in to. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

2 · Deadlines panel

3 · EdgeTodo (Mac)

Put your deadlines in EdgeTodo, the to-do list on the edge of your Mac screen. New deadlines appear there by themselves, and get ticked off when you submit.

Stays on this Mac — Bloom talks to EdgeTodo directly, nothing goes over the internet. In EdgeTodo, keep Settings → Import → Sync deadlines from Bloom on.
